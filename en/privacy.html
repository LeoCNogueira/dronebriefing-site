Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Drone Briefing is an iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch app that helps you decide whether you can fly a drone at a place and time. It is designed to work on your device, with no account and no tracking.

Data we collect

None. Drone Briefing has no accounts, shows no ads, uses no analytics or tracking, and sends the developer no information about you or how you use the app.

Location

With your permission, the app uses the device's location to show the weather and rules where you are. The coordinate is rounded to about 1 km before it leaves the device and goes only to Apple Weather (WeatherKit) for the forecast and, when the place is in the United States, to the public FAA, NWS, aviationweather.gov and National Park Service services listed below. The places you save stay on the device. Address search and the map use Apple Maps.

Like any internet request, these services also receive the connection's IP address, under their own policies.

Pilot wallet and pilot central

The photo, name, ID numbers, documents, insurance policies, aircraft, protocols and flight hours you enter are stored only on the device (and in the device's own iCloud backup, if you use it). DJI Fly flight records are read on the device, only when you pick them, and the app never signs in to DJI. None of this is sent to the developer. When you share a receipt or the PDF dossier, you send it yourself, to whomever you choose.

Widgets, Apple Watch and Siri

The widgets and the Watch app use a copy of the forecast kept on the device and sent to your Watch over Apple's connection between the two. Siri shortcuts run on the device.

Purchases

The Drone Briefing Pro subscription and purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store, under the Apple Privacy Policy. The developer receives no payment details or personal information. The app only asks the App Store whether Pro is active.

This website

This website uses no cookies, ads or analytics. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives the browser's IP address when serving them.

Your rights

Because the data stays on your device, you control it: delete it in the app or by deleting the app. For questions about data protection laws such as Brazil's LGPD or about this policy, write to the contact below.
