Support

Need help or found something wrong? Write to support@internetmidia.com with your device model, iOS version and, if you can, a screenshot. We answer in English or Portuguese.

The verdict

Where does the answer come from? The app compares wind at flight height, gusts, rain, visibility, temperature, Kp and weather warnings with the limits of the chosen drone and type of flight. Tap a factor to see what weighed in.

Does the verdict authorize the flight? No. It is guidance for planning. Authorization still comes from the aviation authority (LAANC in the US, SARPAS in Brazil), and the final decision is the pilot's.

Is the wind above 10 m exact? It is estimated. The forecast is given at 10 m and projected to flight height with a different profile by day and night. Near hills, buildings and the coast the real wind may differ.

Location and places

The app doesn't show where I am. Check Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Location Services ▸ Drone Briefing. Without location, save a place with search.

Times in another time zone. Times follow the time zone of the place shown, not the device's, so the flight window matches the local clock.

In the United States (version 1.2)

Where does the US airspace come from? From the FAA: the UAS Data Exchange (LAANC grid, airspace classes, special use and national security areas, stadiums) and the TFR list, with each TFR's times. The app asks the FAA about a rounded area of about 3 km and checks your exact point on the iPhone.

What does "LAANC up to 50 ft" mean? That is the most LAANC can authorize at that grid cell. The app doesn't authorize: tap Ask for LAANC to open the FAA's list of approved providers or FAADroneZone. A cell at 0 ft needs FAADroneZone.

The verdict says the data is unavailable. When the FAA can't be reached and nothing is saved for the place, the verdict stays at "fly with care" and never reads as clear. Try again with the internet on; answers are kept for 24 hours.

Weather warnings and METAR. NWS warnings for the place appear with the verdict. The nearest airport's METAR (within 10 NM) is checked against the Part 107 minimums: 3 statute miles of visibility and 500 ft below the clouds.

Drone Briefing Pro

How do I subscribe? Tap a feature with the PRO badge or Pilot ▸ Drone Briefing Pro. The yearly plan starts with 7 days free; lifetime is a single payment.

New device. Open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchases, with the Apple Account used for the purchase.

How do I cancel? In Settings ▸ your name ▸ Subscriptions ▸ Drone Briefing. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period and it won't renew. Refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Widgets, Siri and Apple Watch

The widget is out of date. Widgets use the latest forecast downloaded by the app, renewed every 3 hours. Opening the app updates it right away.

The Watch shows no verdict. The Watch app gets the forecast from the iPhone. Open Drone Briefing on the iPhone with the Watch nearby. The Watch is part of Pro.

Siri. Say "Can I fly now with Drone Briefing?" or "Best window today in Drone Briefing" after opening the app once.

Pilot wallet and flight hours

Inspection mode. In the wallet, tap Inspection mode to show your details and the SARPAS lookup QR code on a screen that can't be changed. It works offline.

DJI Fly hours. In Pilot ▸ Flight hours, pick the DJIFlightRecord_*.txt files from the DJI Fly folder (in the Files app or copied from the remote controller). The app reads the duration on the device and never signs in to DJI.
