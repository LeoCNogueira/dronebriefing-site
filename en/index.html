Questions

Does the verdict replace LAANC or SARPAS?

No. Drone Briefing is a planning tool. Flights that need authorization still need it from the aviation authority; the app links to it.

How does the app know the wind at 120 m?

The Apple Weather forecast is given at 10 m. The app projects it to your flight height with a profile that changes by day and night, and tells you values above 10 m are estimated.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account, no ads and no tracking. Only the place's coordinate, rounded to about 1 km, goes to Apple Weather for the forecast.

Which country's rules does it use?

The rules of the country where you fly, never your phone's language or region. Brazil has the most detail: DECEA, ANAC, SARPAS and INMET.

How do I cancel?

On iPhone, in Settings ▸ your name ▸ Subscriptions ▸ Drone Briefing. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period and it won't renew.
