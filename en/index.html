Questions

Does the verdict replace LAANC or SARPAS?

No. Drone Briefing is a planning tool. Flights that need authorization still need it from the aviation authority; the app links to it.

How does the app know the wind at 400 ft?

The Apple Weather forecast is given at 33 ft (10 m). The app projects it to your flight height with a profile that changes by day and night, and tells you values above 33 ft are estimated.

Where do the airspace zones come from?

In Brazil, from DECEA's public GeoAISWEB service: aerodromes, runways, heliports, restricted areas and helicopter corridors, with the distances of ICA 100-40/2026. Outside Brazil the app shows the general rules of the country and links to its official tools.

Do I need an account? What about my location?

No account, no ads, no tracking. Only the place's coordinate, rounded to about 1 km, goes to Apple Weather for the forecast. Zones are worked out on the iPhone, and your documents stay on it.

Does it work offline?

The zone map, the rules and the pilot wallet work offline. The forecast needs the internet to update; widgets show the last one downloaded.

How do I cancel?

On iPhone, in Settings ▸ your name ▸ Subscriptions ▸ Drone Briefing. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period and it won't renew.
